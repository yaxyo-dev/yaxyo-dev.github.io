Troop Crates

A Roblox game about opening crates, collecting troops and surviving a lava obstacle course.

The loop

You buy crates with coins and pull troops of six rarities. Equipped troops earn coins every second, and coins buy better crates. The three Secret troops are the rarest of all: just a 0.2% chance from the Epic Crate.

Reasons to come back

Every unique troop you discover adds 2% to your income for good. Rebirth resets your coins in exchange for +50% income, and its cost triples each time. Daily rewards build a streak of up to seven days.

Lava Run

A 10-stage obstacle course over lava. Fall, and you have 90 seconds to revive at your last checkpoint. Finishing pays out at most once every 10 minutes, so the course can’t be farmed.

What the server does

The client only asks. The server checks the cooldown and the player’s coins, rolls the troop and tells the client what to reveal. Robux purchases go through ProcessReceipt.

Protecting progress

Loading and saving retry up to three times. If a player’s data can’t be loaded, they play on fresh data that is never saved, so a temporary error can’t overwrite real progress. A shutdown hook saves everyone before a server closes.

How it’s organised

Every troop, price and drop rate lives in one GameConfig module, so balancing never touches game logic. Five more scripts handle models, server logic, world building, the interface and the troops that follow each player, which are drawn on the client to stay smooth.
