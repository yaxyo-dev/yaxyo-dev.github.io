The job of this page

Parents and students comparing language centres want proof and a low-risk first step. The first screen pairs the promise with a panel of IELTS band scores and one main button: book a free trial lesson.

The look

Navy and gold with a crest-style mark, so it feels like a school and not a shop. “Natija” (results) is the only gold italic word in the headline, because it’s the one thing the page needs to be remembered for.

How it’s built

One HTML file of about 8 KB, with a few lines of JavaScript for the sign-up form. Courses are cards showing the level, schedule and monthly price. In the real version, sign-ups go straight to the centre’s Telegram.

What’s still a placeholder

The band scores, prices and phone number are examples, and the page labels them that way. They get replaced with real student results and certificates.
